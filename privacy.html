Legal — Privacy Policy

Your Data.
Your Rules.

A plain-language explanation of what IKANDY collects, what it doesn't, and how you can control or delete your data at any time.

Version 2.0 · Effective 2026-07-31
Quick Summary
  • Steam builds send diagnostics and basic usage analytics by default. Both can be turned off any time in Settings → Privacy. Beta builds keep telemetry off by default.
  • Analytics are tied to your Steam account ID (a number). Steam never gives us your real name, email, or payment details.
  • We derive a coarse region (country level) from your connection to see where IKANDY is used. Your IP address itself is not stored.
  • We never collect: your music, listening history, audio content, file names, account credentials, or your real identity.
  • You control everything. View your ID, toggle diagnostics and analytics, and permanently delete your data from inside the app.

01

What This Document Covers

This Privacy Policy explains how IKANDY ("the Software"), operated by L&R Entertainment LLC ("we", "us"), collects and processes data when you install and use the desktop application. It reflects the data practices implemented in IKANDY version 1.0 and later.

This policy mirrors the data sections of the License Agreement in plain language. The License Agreement is the binding contract for your use of the Software; where the two documents describe data practices differently, this Privacy Policy controls.

02

Data Required to Use IKANDY

Steam builds. IKANDY is sold and installed through Steam. To confirm ownership and unlock the tier you purchased, the app talks to Steam and, on our side, records the minimum needed:

DataWhat it isWhy we need it
Steam account ID The numeric ID Steam exposes for your account. Steam does not give us your real name, email, or payment details. Confirms you own IKANDY and which tier to unlock, and lets us tie support and bug reports to a purchase.
App version, platform Strings like "1.2.0" and "win32". Routes you to the correct logic for your build.
EULA acceptance record Hash of the agreement you accepted plus a timestamp. Records that you agreed to the current terms.

Beta builds (pre-release). Beta access validates against our server with a random Beta ID (a UUID generated on first install) and a one-way SHA-256 machine fingerprint hash of your MAC address, hostname, and CPU model. The original values cannot be recovered from the hash, and none of it identifies you personally.

03

Diagnostics & Usage Analytics

IKANDY has two telemetry channels. In Steam builds both are on by default so we can find bugs and understand where and how the app is used; you can turn either off at any time in Settings → Privacy. In beta builds both remain off by default.

Usage Analytics

When on, IKANDY records:

  • Launch timestamp, app version, and platform
  • Session duration (recorded on app close)
  • Feature usage events: which features get used, never what you play or make with them
  • A coarse region (country level), derived from your connection at the moment of ingestion; the IP address itself is not stored
  • Your Steam account ID (Steam builds) or anonymous install ID (beta builds)

We do not collect playback choices, music library contents, song titles, or the contents of anything you create.

Crash & Bug Diagnostics

When on, IKANDY sends a technical report if the application crashes, and diagnostic traces when you report a bug. Before anything leaves your device it is automatically sanitized:

  • Your Windows username is replaced with <user>
  • File paths from your user directory are stripped or generalized
  • Development paths are masked

The resulting report contains the error type, a sanitized stack trace, recent technical log lines leading up to the problem, app version, platform, OS version, GPU model, and the same account or install ID as above. Sanitization happens on your device before transmission; we never see your real username or full file paths. Verbose diagnostic trace files that some debug options write stay on your machine unless you choose to send them.

04

What We Do Not Collect

IKANDY never transmits any of the following to any server:

  • The contents of audio playing on your computer
  • The names of songs, artists, or albums you play
  • Your music library or playlists
  • Your Spotify, foobar2000, VLC, or any other account credentials
  • Your real name or email address
  • The names of files on your computer
  • Screenshots or video
  • Biometric data of any kind

Your Steam account ID is a number issued by Valve. Receiving it does not give us your real name, email address, or payment information.

05

About Your IP Address

Like every internet service, IKANDY makes HTTPS connections that include your IP address at the network level. We do not store your IP address in our database tables and we do not log it for analytics purposes. For usage analytics we derive a coarse region (country level) from the connection at the moment a record arrives, then discard the address; only the region is kept.

Your IP address may appear transiently in standard Supabase infrastructure logs — these are governed by Supabase's privacy policy.

06

Where Your Data Is Stored

All data collected by IKANDY is stored on Supabase, a SOC 2 Type II-certified backend platform. Your data is held under their privacy policy and security commitments.

We do not share, sell, or rent your data to third parties for advertising, marketing, or any other purpose.

07

Website Analytics

This Website

The ikandy.app website uses Google Analytics 4 to count visits and understand which pages and buttons get used. This measures the website only; it is separate from the app, which works fully offline. Google Analytics 4 does not log or store individual IP addresses, and we do not use it for advertising or remarketing. Interaction events carry no personal information: a button click sends only the button's visible text. Data is processed by Google under Google's privacy policy. Standard content or script blockers will prevent analytics from loading, and the site works fine without it.

The Arcade

The IKANDY Arcade is a free browser mini-game site. It never asks for your email, name, or any account. To put you on the leaderboards it uses:

  • An anonymous ID and handle. A random UUID and a randomly generated handle (like "Mildly-Cursed Earl #25429"), stored in your browser's localStorage. Neither is linked to your real identity.
  • Score submissions. When a game ends, your anonymous ID, handle, score, run duration, an input count (used only for anti-cheat validation), and a random session ID are sent to our backend (Supabase). Your handle, score, and date appear on public leaderboards visible to other players.
  • Spread the Word history (contest now closed). While the contest ran, URLs you submitted were fetched by our server once to verify they mentioned IKANDY, and are stored with your anonymous ID and handle. Submissions are no longer accepted.

The arcade pages also load the same Google Analytics described above. To remove your arcade data or leaderboard entries, email support@ikandy.app with your handle.

Your Rights

Access

Open Settings → Privacy in IKANDY to view your anonymous ID at any time.

Withdraw Consent

Disable either telemetry toggle at any time. Future events will not be transmitted. Past records remain unless you also request deletion.

Delete Your Data

Use the "Delete my data from IKANDY servers" button in Settings → Privacy. This permanently:

  • Deletes all usage and crash report records tied to your installation
  • Anonymizes your beta installation record (removes personal identifiers while keeping the row to prevent beta-slot recycling)
  • Generates a fresh anonymous ID locally so future sessions start clean

Rejoin After Deletion

If you delete your data and later wish to rejoin the IKANDY beta, contact support@ikandy.app with your Beta ID. Reactivation is processed manually and typically completes within several days.

08

Children's Privacy

IKANDY is not directed at children under the age of 16. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 16. If you believe a child has used IKANDY and provided data, contact us immediately and we will remove the relevant records.
