User guide/AI scene generation

AI scene generation

Bring your own key. Generate, fix, and tune scenes with a supported provider.

An AI that writes scenes

IKANDY can generate new visual scenes with AI, and can repair or tune imported shaders in the AI shader studio. It is bring-your-own-key: you connect an account from a supported provider and pay that provider directly. IKANDY does not sell separate AI credits.

Your key stays yours

The credential is stored using the operating system's secure storage. IKANDY uses it to authenticate requests to the provider you choose; provider terms and data handling apply.

What it can do

  • Generate: describe a look and get a runnable scene.
  • AI Fix: paste a shader that will not compile and let the sandbox suggest a repair after a compile check.
  • AI Tune: ask for adjustments to a working scene, in plain language.

Costs

Provider charges, availability, and estimates can change. Review the estimate shown in IKANDY and your provider's terms before you submit a job.

Guided AI scene generation ships in the Pro tier. See pricing; the Steam page is authoritative.
