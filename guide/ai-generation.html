User guide/AI scene generation

AI scene generation

Bring your own key. Generate, fix, and tune scenes at provider cost.

An AI that writes scenes, at cost

IKANDY can generate new visual scenes with AI, and can repair or tune imported shaders in the AI shader studio. It is bring-your-own-key: you paste a key from your AI provider and pay them directly. IKANDY adds no markup and sells no credits.

Your key stays yours

The key is encrypted with the operating system's secure storage the moment you paste it. The app's rendering layer never sees the raw key, and it never leaves your machine except to talk to your provider.

What it can do

  • Generate: describe a look and get a runnable scene.
  • AI Fix: paste a shader that will not compile and let the sandbox repair it. The sandbox is designed to catch errors safely so they don't take the app down.
  • AI Tune: ask for adjustments to a working scene, in plain language.

Costs

Because you pay the provider directly, costs are the provider's list prices. Typical fixes and tunes cost cents. IKANDY shows a live cost estimate before you run a job, using current provider pricing.

Guided AI scene generation ships in the Pro tier. See pricing; the Steam page is authoritative.
