Logo maker

Image Studio: text or image, chrome and matte finishes, live on any visual.

Image Studio: make the logo

The logo maker builds a broadcast-style overlay that rides on top of any running visual: your name, your mark, live on the output.

Create

Start from text or bring an image from your library. A studio preview backdrop helps you judge it, and it is preview only: your logo stays transparent and the backdrop is never baked into the asset.

Finishes

Two live material finishes ship today: Chrome, liquid metal that catches the scene, and Black, a matte powder-coat. Each asset also carries its own audio-reactivity control, so the logo can breathe with the music or hold still.

Save and apply

Apply Live puts it on the output immediately. Save as New keeps a version; originals and templates are protected from overwrite. On STAGE, the Logo button opens the Studio directly and lights up whenever an overlay is live, so you always know it is on.
