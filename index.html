U1 SIGNAL PATH

An instrument, not a screensaver.

IKANDY is built like the gear it sits next to: a real audio engine that captures one app's sound and ignores the rest, a real render pipeline with an HDR post chain, and outputs that plug into the rest of your rig. It runs fully offline, on your GPU, and you buy it once.

And when you do want wallpaper, it's the best wallpaper you've ever had. Any music-reactive preset can run as your Windows desktop, moving with your music behind your icons while you work. One library of presets, everywhere you point it: your desktop, a second monitor, an OBS overlay, a projector wall, a stage.

U2 WHO IT'S FOR

One app. Three rooms.

CH 1

Listeners

Open it on a second monitor, hit play in Spotify, YouTube, foobar2000, VLC, anything on your PC, and walk away. IKANDY auto-cycles through hundreds of scenes, scrolls synced lyrics, and reacts to your music, not your Discord pings.

CH 2

DJs & VJs

A node in the rig, not a walled garden. Spout out into Resolume or OBS. Spout in to post-process any feed with a rack of live effect scenes. Per-app audio capture locks onto the booth signal and ignores everything else. MIDI, OSC, and a console built to be performed.

CH 3

Shader artists

Paste a Shadertoy-style fragment and it runs. GLSL or WGSL, with audio uniforms, mouse, and a deep HDR post chain doing the bloom and grain for you. Your work, fullscreen at full frame rate, with your name on it.
