Who it's for

One app. Three kinds of people.

Listeners

Open it on a second monitor, hit play in Spotify, YouTube, foobar2000, VLC, anything on your PC, and walk away. IKANDY auto-cycles through hundreds of scenes, scrolls synced lyrics, and reacts to your music, not your Discord pings.

VJs & DJs

It's a node in the rig, not a walled garden. Spout out into Resolume or OBS, Spout in to post-process any feed with 14 live effect scenes, and per-process audio capture that locks onto the booth signal and ignores everything else.

Shader artists

Paste a Shadertoy-style fragment and it runs. GLSL or WGSL, with audio uniforms, mouse, and a 9-pass HDR post chain doing the bloom and grain for you. Your work, fullscreen at full frame rate, with your name on it.

What's inside

The short list.

  • MILKDROP ENGINE868 community presets running on Butterchurn, the WebGL port of Winamp's legendary MilkDrop.
  • AI SCENE GENERATORType a prompt, get a working audio-reactive shader. Keep it, tweak it, or roll again.
  • SPOUT, BOTH WAYSBe the source, the destination, or the effect in the middle of any video toolchain.
  • PER-PROCESS AUDIOReact to one app's sound alone. Not the game. Not the notification.
  • SYNCED LYRICSPulled live and drawn on screen. Accidental karaoke machine.
